# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers who want to understand Haikal Abizar's profile and work history, then assess his fit for a role.

## Product Purpose

Present Haikal Abizar's professional profile and work history so recruiters and hiring managers can assess his fit for a role.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The portfolio presents profile information, work history, education, skills, a downloadable resume, and project case studies for WMS, DICE, and TRAILOO.
- The interface supports Indonesian and English.
- A blog is not a current requirement.
- Do not publish internal company details. Do not make claims or include figures without evidence.

## Evidence on Hand

- Work history and education content in `app/page.tsx`.
- WMS, DICE, and TRAILOO project descriptions and illustrative interface previews in `app/page.tsx` and `app/components/ProjectStack.tsx`.
- Resume at `public/Haikal-Abizar-Resume.pdf`.
- Portrait asset at `public/haikal-portrait-editorial.png`.

## Product Principles

- Make it easy for hiring teams to understand the candidate's profile and work history.
- Keep project descriptions accurate and grounded in evidence.
- Protect internal company information in public case studies.
